# Sample Campaign Briefs

Ten realistic briefs for exercising the generation workflow. Paste one into the **Raw Marketing
Brief** field on `/generate`, pick the suggested selections, and generate.

> **These are fictional test fixtures.** They reference real brands, product lines and athletes
> to make the copy realistic, but the campaigns, dates, SKUs, discounts and messaging are
> invented. Nothing here is affiliated with, endorsed by, or sourced from Nike, adidas, PUMA or
> any named individual. Do not publish generated output based on these briefs.

## How to use them

The seeded database only contains the demo brands *AeroFlex* and *Northline*. Either:

- leave **Brand / Product** empty — the CTA falls back to `SHOP THE COLLECTION`; or
- add the brand and product first under **Brands & Products** (admin role), which makes the CTA
  resolve to `SHOP <PRODUCT>`.

Briefs that name an athlete exercise the extraction agent's public-figure path. That agent only
records a person when the brief explicitly labels them as an athlete, ambassador or endorser, so
those lines are worded deliberately.

## 1. Nike Air Max — Black Friday

**Channel:** Email · **Audience:** Performance Seekers · **Language:** English

```
Black Friday is our biggest running moment of the year. We are promoting the Nike Air Max
running range at up to 40% off from midnight Friday through Cyber Monday. SKU AM90BF24 covers
the core colorways.

The Air Max line is known for visible Air cushioning, lightweight mesh uppers, breathable
comfort and everyday durability. This campaign targets runners who have been waiting for the
right moment to upgrade a worn pair.

Key message: Big cushioning. Bigger savings.

Use an urgent, energetic tone. Emphasise the limited window, free returns and that popular sizes
sell out first. Do not overstate the discount: say "up to 40%", never a flat 40%.
```

## 2. adidas Ultraboost — Christmas gifting

**Channel:** Email · **Audience:** General / All · **Language:** English

```
Promote the adidas Ultraboost as the running gift that does not get returned. Campaign runs
December 1 to December 22, with guaranteed delivery before Christmas on orders placed by
December 18. SKU UB24XMAS.

Ultraboost is built around responsive cushioning, a knit upper for breathable comfort, and
durability that holds up through winter training. Gift wrapping and extended January returns are
included.

Key message: Give the run they will actually love.

Use a warm, festive tone. Speak to the gift buyer rather than the runner. Highlight the delivery
cut-off date and the extended returns window, both of which reduce purchase anxiety.
```

## 3. PUMA RS-X — new colorway drop

**Channel:** Mobile · **Audience:** Trendsetters · **Language:** English

```
We are dropping four new PUMA RS-X colorways on Friday at 10am, limited to 500 pairs per
colorway. SKU RSX2024DROP. Early access opens two hours ahead for app users.

The RS-X is a chunky retro silhouette with bold layered panelling, modern design details and
all-day comfort. This is a style-first drop, not a performance story.

Key message: Loud by design.

Use a confident, fashion-forward tone. Lead with exclusivity and the limited count. Push app
early access hard. Keep it short — this is a push notification and in-app moment.
```

## 4. Nike Basketball — LeBron James signature line

**Channel:** Email · **Audience:** Enthusiasts · **Language:** English

```
Launch the latest Nike LeBron signature basketball shoe, endorsed by LeBron James. SKU
LJ22PE001. Available in three team colorways plus one player-exclusive edition.

The shoe is built for explosive performance: responsive cushioning through the forefoot,
lockdown support, durable traction for indoor courts, and modern design detailing throughout.

Key message: Built for the ones who never sit down.

Use a bold, reverential tone aimed at fans who follow the signature line closely. Reference
craft and construction detail — this audience reads the spec sheet. Do not invent statistics,
career claims or quotes.
```

## 5. adidas Football — Lionel Messi boots

**Channel:** Email · **Audience:** Enthusiasts · **Language:** English

```
We are launching a new adidas football boot in the signature range endorsed by Lionel Messi.
SKU AD-MES-F50. Firm ground and artificial grass versions both available at launch.

The boot focuses on lightweight speed, a soft touch upper for close control, responsive
cushioning underfoot and durability across a full season. A limited signature colorway ships
with a numbered box.

Key message: Made for the moments that decide games.

Use an inspiring, premium tone for collectors and serious players. Emphasise craft and the
limited numbered edition. Avoid any claim about match performance we cannot support.
```

## 6. PUMA Football — Neymar Jr campaign

**Channel:** Mobile · **Audience:** Enthusiasts · **Language:** English

```
Promote the PUMA football range featuring Neymar Jr as brand ambassador. SKU PM-NJ-FUT01. The
campaign runs alongside the new season kit launch.

The range covers boots and training apparel built for speed, agility, breathable comfort and

Key message: Play with flair.

superline, pre-heading and headline all need to work on a small screen.
```

## 7. Nike Running — New Year fitness reset

**Channel:** Email · **Audience:** Performance Seekers · **Language:** English

```
January fitness reset campaign for the Nike running range, running January 1 to January 31.
Bundle offer: 20% off when a shoe is purchased with any two apparel items. SKU NR-JAN-BUNDLE.

The range covers everyday trainers and tempo shoes with responsive cushioning, breathable
uppers, lightweight construction and the durability to survive a full training block. Targeting
lapsed runners restarting after the holidays, plus first-time runners committing to a resolution.

Key message: Start where you are. Go further than last year.

Use an encouraging, non-judgemental tone. Avoid guilt, body-shaming or any language implying the
reader is unfit. Focus on the restart, not the setback.
```

## 8. adidas Originals Samba — lifestyle

**Channel:** Mobile · **Audience:** Trendsetters · **Language:** English

```
Promote the adidas Originals Samba as an everyday lifestyle sneaker, not a performance shoe.
SKU AO-SAMBA-CLS. Six core colorways available year round.

The Samba is a low-profile terrace classic: slim silhouette, suede overlays, gum sole, modern
design that pairs with almost anything. Comfort and versatility across a full day on your feet.

Key message: The classic that never left.

Use a calm, understated tone. This audience dislikes hype language — no exclamation marks, no
"must-have". Lead with heritage and versatility.
```

## 9. PUMA — Cyber Monday clearance

**Channel:** SMS · **Audience:** General / All · **Language:** English

```
Cyber Monday clearance across PUMA footwear and apparel. Up to 50% off, one day only, ending
11:59pm Monday. SKU PM-CM-CLEAR. Free shipping on all orders, no minimum.

The clearance spans running, training and lifestyle styles, covering comfort, durability,
versatility and modern design across the range. Stock is limited to what remains in each size.

Key message: One day. Everything down.

Use an urgent, direct tone. This is an SMS: one sentence, the offer, the deadline, and a short
action. Do not exceed the SMS character budget.
```

## 10. Nike — Holiday countdown, last-minute gifting

**Channel:** Email · **Audience:** General / All · **Language:** English

```
Last-minute holiday gifting push for Nike, running December 19 to December 24. Digital gift
cards deliver instantly; physical orders are no longer guaranteed before Christmas. SKU
NK-GIFT-DIGI.

Position gift cards and easy-to-size items — apparel, accessories, socks — as the safe choice
when time has run out. Emphasise instant delivery, comfort, quality and the fact that the
recipient picks their own size and style.

Key message: Still time. Still perfect.

Use a light, slightly self-aware tone that acknowledges the reader left it late without mocking
them. Be explicit that physical delivery before Christmas is no longer guaranteed.
```
